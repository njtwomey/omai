# Libraries next to omai's other domains: offline RL and imitation, control and filtering, change points and motifs,
# optimisation (evolutionary, convex modelling, optimal transport, optimisers), geometry, symbolic algebra, probabilistic logic,
# causal discovery, uncertainty and conformal prediction, forecasting zoos, recommenders, data checks and drift reports, game
# theory, sketches, subgroup discovery and applied statistics.
# Introspected from the installed environment: control, filterpy, ruptures, stumpy, pysindy, cma, pymoo, cvxpy, pot, optax,
# shapely, sympy, causallearn, mapie, nashpy, openspiel (game names and algorithm modules), datasketch, pysubgroup, pingouin.
# From memory: d3rlpy, imitation, pykalman, nevergrad, trimesh, problog, gcastle, uncertainty_toolbox, neuralforecast, darts,
# gluonts, surprise, implicit, lightfm, recbole, deepchecks, evidently.
# Libraries with hundreds of names (sympy, cvxpy, pymoo problems, POT, optax, shapely, causallearn internals) were judged by
# group, not name by name; the groups are described in the notes. diffprivlib is installed but cannot be imported in this
# environment (it needs a private scikit-learn symbol), so it was not enumerated; its mechanisms are covered under Opacus.
