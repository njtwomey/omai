# The torch ecosystem beyond torch itself: model architectures (torchvision.models enumerated from the installed package;
# timm from memory), audio (librosa enumerated, torchaudio from memory), text pipelines (torchtext from memory),
# augmentation (kornia, albumentations from memory) and model inspection (torchinfo, torch.fx, ONNX concepts from memory).
# Pretrained weights, detection heads trained on COCO and video models are out of scope on purpose. What is measured is
# whether the architecture exists as a small, trainable, describable network. omai has the blocks (nn/blocks/vision.ts:
# ResNet basic and bottleneck blocks, squeeze-excitation, ConvNeXt, MBConv, U-Net stages, feature pyramid; transformer
# blocks and patch embedding; mixers; state-space layers) and `nn#describe` and `nn#summary`; it has no assembled
# named networks (ResNet-18, VGG, ViT, Swin, DenseNet, Inception).
