# Structured prediction and sequence labelling. sklearn-crfsuite, python-crfsuite and pytorch-crf were enumerated from the
# installed packages (`crf_libraries`); CRF++, AllenNLP, flair, pystruct, seqeval and the wider literature are from memory
# (`crf_tools`: names and features, not an API inventory).
# Verified in the source: pgm/models/crf.ts (linearChainCrf, crfScore, crfLogLikelihood, crfMarginals, crfDecode,
# crfppCrf, crfppObservation, crfParameters, crfFeatureCounts, crfExpectedFeatureCounts, fitCrf, generalCrf, fitGeneralCrf
# with exact or loopy BP), pgm/models/crf-training.ts (the solver slot, with the structured perceptron, the averaged
# perceptron and passive-aggressive with cost-augmented decoding as modes; c1 and c2 penalties), text/templates.ts (CRF++
# and Wapiti templates), text/attribute-templates.ts, metrics/sequence-labelling.ts (seqeval-style entity scores),
# text (CKY, Earley, PCFG training, arc-standard and Eisner dependency parsing), pgm (HMM with Baum-Welch, HSMM).
