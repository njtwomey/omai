## What omai has today

Read from `packages/engine/src/lib` (the exports and the source, not the index alone). Covariance estimation lives in one folder and is spread
over about ten others; nothing below is new, it is the inventory the verdicts were made against.

| where                                                                       | what is there                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | what it is missing, by the references below                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `covariance/shrinkage.ts`                                                   | `covarianceEstimator` (also the `CovarianceEstimator` class and `shrinkageAlgorithm`): empirical, fixed-intensity shrunk, Ledoit-Wolf and OAS, with location, covariance, precision (pseudo-inverse), the shrinkage intensity and log-determinant on the tape; verbs `mahalanobis` and `score` (mean Gaussian log likelihood); `squaredMahalanobis`, `mahalanobisDistances`, an `anomalyScores` exposition | one target only (a scaled identity); the algorithm is one step, so the intensity is not a path or a slider; no choice among estimators by held-out likelihood                                      |
| `covariance/robust.ts`                                                      | `minCovDet` and `fastMcdAlgorithm` (random starts screened by C-steps, each C-step a traced step; consistency factor and chi-squared reweighting as scikit-learn), `cStepAlgorithm`, `ellipticEnvelope` over MCD or any of the shrinkage estimators                                                                                                                                                        | the other robust scatter estimators: M, Tyler, t-MLE, OGK, MVE, S, MM, Stahel-Donoho, DetMCD, MRCD, spatial sign; cellwise methods                                                                 |
| `covariance/graphical-lasso.ts`                                             | `graphicalLassoAlgorithm` (column-wise coordinate descent through `optim`'s lasso, duality gap, objective and edge count on the tape), `graphicalLasso`, `graphicalLassoEstimator` (the exposition is a `regularisationPath` of partial correlations over a penalty grid)                                                                                                                                  | choice of the penalty (CV, EBIC, StARS), warm-started path, adaptive and non-convex penalties, joint, time-varying and latent variants, neighbourhood estimators, a second-order (QUIC-style) mode |
| `stats/covariance.ts`, `stats/correlation.ts`                               | `empiricalCovariance`, `shrunkCovariance`, `ledoitWolf`, `oas`; `cov`, `corrcoef` (numpy's frequency and reliability weights), `partialCorrelation`, `pearson`, `spearman`, `kendallTau` with tests, `distanceCorrelation`                                                                                                                                                                                 | no NaN handling or pairwise-complete option; correlation matrices of ranks only as `dists#kendallTauMatrix`                                                                                        |
| `stats/running.ts`, `stats/robust.ts`, `sampling/hamiltonian/adaptation.ts` | univariate Welford moments with parallel merge, `ewmMean`/`ewmVar`/`ewmStd`; Huber and Tukey location M-estimates, trimmed and winsorised moments, RANSAC; a streaming covariance with Stan-style shrinkage towards the identity inside the mass-matrix adaptation                                                                                                                                         | no multivariate running covariance or exponentially weighted covariance as an exported estimator                                                                                                   |
| `decomposition/`                                                            | `pca` (exact, power iteration, whitening), `incrementalPca`, `sparsePca`, `probabilisticPca`, `factorAnalysis` (EM, varimax and quartimax), `latentCovariance` (the `W W' + psi` matrix), `cca`, `fastIca`, `pcr`                                                                                                                                                                                          | robust PCA (ROBPCA, spherical), principal component pursuit, promax; the factor-model covariance assembled for a given set of factors                                                              |
| `dists/multivariate/`                                                       | `multivariateNormal` (covariance, precision or Cholesky), `lowRankMultivariateNormal`, `wishart`, `inverseWishart`, `lkj`, `lkjCholesky`, `matrixNormal`, Gaussian, Student t and Archimedean copulas with `fitCopula`, `wasserstein2Gaussian`; `conjugatePosterior` has the inverse-Wishart and Wishart updates for a normal with known mean                                                              | the multivariate Student t, G-Wishart, a Wishart process, an inverse-Wishart shrinkage estimator by name                                                                                           |
| `stats/tests/multivariate.ts`, `stats/missing.ts`                           | `boxM`, `hotellingT2`, `manova`, `mardia`, `bartlett`, `levene`, `littleMcarTest`, `partialCorrelationTest` (in `pgm/learning`)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | sphericity (Mauchly, John), one-sample and identity tests, Jennrich, Fisher-z comparisons of correlations, KMO, high-dimensional tests                                                             |
| `numerics/linalg`, `optim/proximal.ts`                                      | `cholesky` with `choleskyUpdate`, `choleskyDowndate`, `choleskyJitter`, `choleskyLogdet`, `isPositiveDefinite`, `sqrtm`, `logm`, `expm`, `matrixPower`, `eigh`, `pinv`, `toeplitz`, `circulant`, `solveToeplitz`, `solveSylvester`, Lyapunov solvers, `softImpute`, `lowRankApproximation`, `projectPsd`, `proxNuclear`; `numerics/geometry#covarianceEllipse`                                             | nearest correlation matrix (Dykstra), the SPD metrics and means, Haar sampling                                                                                                                     |
| `linear/`, `timeseries/`, `mixture/`, `learn/`                              | `olsSummary` with HC0 to HC3, `glmCovariance`, `ar1Covariance`, `covarianceFactor`, GLS; VAR residual covariance, univariate GARCH family; full, tied, diagonal and spherical mixture covariances, variational Bayesian mixtures with Wishart priors; shrinkage and regularised discriminant analysis; `iterativeImputer`, `knnImputer`                                                                    | HAC and cluster-robust errors for any regression, multivariate GARCH, realised covariance; EM covariance from incomplete data                                                                      |
| `datasets/`, `render`                                                       | `makeSpdMatrix`, `makeSparseSpdMatrix`, `garchReturns`, correlated-feature modifiers; a correlation chart and the graph and heatmap charts in `omai-render`                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | generators that carry a covariance truth for spiked, banded, Toeplitz, factor or graph-structured precision; no Marchenko-Pastur or other random-matrix laws anywhere in `dists`                   |
