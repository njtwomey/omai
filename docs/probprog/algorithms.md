# The target repertoire: a taxonomy of inference algorithms

Part of the [probabilistic programming plan](PLAN.md) (section 4). One row per algorithm or per family of variants. The
columns:
- **Now**: `have` (exists as an engine or kernel and is reachable on programs), `kernel` (exists in `sampling/` or `pgm/` but is not
  reachable on programs or on a model spec), `special` (exists only as a hand-written special case elsewhere), `partial`, `new`.
- **Needs**: what it needs of the core (see plan section 5): `LD` = unconstrained log density with gradient (`potentialEnergy`),
  `FG` = factor graph of exponential-family variables compiled from a model spec, `CT` = conditional tables or conditional draws
  of a site given the rest (block target), `EF` = exponential-family form (`dists/exponential-family.ts`), `H` = effect handlers,
  `AN` = structure analysis (conjugacy, blankets, tree or chain detection).
- **Knobs** become a `Space` (`dict` of `int`, `real`, `choice`, `union` with a mode, per `lib/space`), so the controls are
  automatic and the method can be tuned and raced in lockstep. A slot filled from an option table is written `slot:`.
- **Tape** are the typed log paths of the method's `defineContract` (names follow the existing `mcmc/...`, `ve/...`, `init/...`
  style). `m` marks a message event that the render package's message timeline and structure overlay already draw.
- **Proof**: how it is shown to work (plan section 6): E exact reference, G golden fixture (NumPyro, BlackJAX, ArviZ, pgmpy,
  pyAgrum, scikit-learn, scipy installed in the oracle), A agreement with another engine on the benchmark suite, S simulation-based
  calibration, P property or convergence test.
- **Size** S a day or less, M a few days, L a week or more; **pri** H, M, L.

**In the browser**: every row says `ok` (small models, interactive), `ok+w` (use a worker, chains or particles in parallel) or
`demo` (only toy sizes). Message passing, Laplace and SVI are `ok` almost everywhere; NUTS is `ok+w` for models of up to some
thousands of observations (two timings in the inventory); SBC campaigns, nested sampling in more than a few dimensions and neural
amortisation are `demo`.
