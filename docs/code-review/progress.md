# Progress

Statuses: planned, in progress, complete, dropped. Times are local, 2026-11-07, taken from commit times for finished
work; "about" means reconstructed from the session. Update a row when its state changes.
- **Commit or file**: where the work or its evidence is: the commits of finished work, the file a finding will be written
  to, or the section of `findings.md` or `reports/` a planned item comes from.
- **Notes**: what the process identified: a result, a number, a finding, a reason it is blocked. Findings also get a row
  in `register.md`.
- **Execution notes** (optional): what happened while doing it: an interesting turn, a gotcha, a decision made, a change
  of scope. Decisions also get a row in `decisions.md`; the dated story is in `log.md`.
