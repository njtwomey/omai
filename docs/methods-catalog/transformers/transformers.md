# Transformers (part 6, neural architectures)

The transformer watched: attention stepped on a short sequence, positional encodings (sinusoidal, learned, rotary, ALiBi) and length extrapolation, the block (pre-norm, gated feed-forward, RMS norm) and the attention variants (multi-head, grouped-query, local, linear, sparse), the architecture families (the original encoder-decoder, encoder-only masked models, decoder-only, the vision transformer, the decision transformer), training and analysis at toy size (warm-up, depth and normalisation, grokking), and interpreting transformers (attention maps and rollout, induction heads forming, the logit lens, linear probes, activation patching, superposition), as sessions over `nn`, `explain`, `datasets/sequence-tasks` and the trainer base. Everything runs at toy scale: a few layers and heads on synthetic tasks and tiny corpora; the mechanisms are real, the models are not.

## Structure

Problem methods of the torch world (section 7.2 of `methods/README.md`) under `train/sequence/transformers/`, a folder of the sequence tree beside the recurrent and state-space families and the language models; the stepped attention and the positional study are probe-only methods of the same tree. Decoding and everything a language model does after training (sampling, prompting, adaptation, alignment) is `large-language-models`'; the set transformer and graph transformers are `graph-neural-networks`'.

```
train/sequence/transformers/
  mechanism/        attention stepped, positional encodings and extrapolation, the block, the attention variants
  families/         the original encoder-decoder, a masked encoder, a small decoder, the vision transformer, the decision transformer
  training/         warm-up and schedules, depth and normalisation, grokking, scaling as a see row
  interpretation/   attention maps and rollout, induction heads, the logit lens, linear probes, activation patching, superposition and sparse autoencoders
  shared/           the token task slot, the attention probes, the token strip and next-token records (below)
```

## Methods

Tags on tracks: `s` a per-step scalar, `t` a per-step tensor at summary detail, `t!` a tensor kept in full, `p` a probe computed from the state at any step, `proj` a projection over the tape, `e` an event, `m` a message with a payload. Trained rows step one optimiser update on the trainer base (`train/loss`, `train/gradNorm`, `train/lr`, `eval/<metric>`, `model/params` as checkpoints; `TODO: methods/shared/bases/trainer.ts` over `nn/training/fit.ts`: `fitAlgorithm`); attention maps are `attention/<layer>/<head>` t! for one example at the viewed step, the next-token distribution for a prompt is `model/nextToken` p. Nothing in this chapter is built.

## Examples to make

- Attention on an eight-token sequence: one head's scores and the weighted values, the mask as a knob.

- The next-token distribution for a prompt as training proceeds, and the logit lens over layers at the viewed step.
