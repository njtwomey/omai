# Generative models (part 6, neural architectures)

Deep generative models trained and sampled: autoencoders and variational autoencoders (β, posterior collapse, importance weighting, vector quantisation), autoregressive models (MADE, a small PixelCNN), normalising flows (planar and radial, coupling, autoregressive, continuous), generative adversarial networks (the objective as a mode, gradient penalties, spectral normalisation, mode collapse), energy-based models (score matching, noise-contrastive estimation, Langevin sampling with a replay buffer), diffusion models (the forward process, the denoiser's parameterisation, the sampler as a mode, the SDE and ODE views, guidance), flow matching with few-step sampling, and the evaluation of samples, as sessions over `nn`, `losses`, `dists`, `sampling`, `stochastic`, `dynamics/ode` and the trainer base, on 2-D densities with exact log densities and on tiny images.

## Structure

Problem methods of the torch world (section 7.2 of `methods/README.md`) under `train/generative/`, one folder per family; the samplers that run on a trained model (or on the exact score of a toy density, which needs no training) are probe-only or sampling-only methods of the same tree. Hopfield networks and restricted Boltzmann machines are `feedforward-networks`' `energy-based/`; the SDEs and score dynamics the diffusion rows ride on are `probability`'s and `stochastic`'s; autoencoders as representation learners are `representation-learning`'s.

```
train/generative/
  autoencoders/     the autoencoder (its regulariser as a mode), the VAE (β, likelihood, IWAE), posterior collapse, vector quantisation
  autoregressive/   MADE and NADE on binary data, a small PixelCNN, autoregressive sampling
  flows/            the flow as a mode (planar, radial, affine and additive coupling, autoregressive), the continuous flow
  adversarial/      the GAN (the objective as a mode), stabilisation, mode collapse and coverage, conditional
  energy-based/     the EBM on 2-D (score matching, NCE, contrastive divergence with Langevin), the classifier as an energy model
  diffusion/        the forward process, the denoiser, the sampler as a mode, the SDE and ODE views, guidance, flow matching
  evaluation/       likelihood against the truth, sample-quality scores, coverage
  shared/           the toy density slot with truth, the tiny-image slot, the sample and field probes, schedules (below)
```

## Methods

Tags on tracks: `s` a per-step scalar, `t` a per-step tensor at summary detail, `t!` a tensor kept in full, `p` a probe computed from the state at any step, `proj` a projection over the tape, `e` an event, `m` a message with a payload. Trained rows step one optimiser update on the trainer base (`train/loss`, `train/gradNorm`, `train/lr`, `eval/<metric>`, `model/params` as checkpoints; `TODO: methods/shared/bases/trainer.ts` over `nn/training/fit.ts`: `fitAlgorithm`); sampling rows step one sampler step. Every model on 2-D data has the same probes: `sample/<k>` p (k samples at the viewed step over the true density), `model/density` p (the learned density or energy on a grid where the model has one), `model/field` p (a vector field: the score, the velocity, the flow's displacement). Nothing in this chapter is built.

## Problems

- The diffusion sampler as a session of its own on the exact score of a toy density is the cheap, high-value page: no training, every sampler as a mode, and the same page serves any trained denoiser afterwards.
- The library has every loss and block and lacks the small sampling layer named above: noise schedules, the DDPM and DDIM samplers as algorithms, guidance, reflow, the VQ and NCE losses, planar and radial flows, masked convolution; `stochastic/diffusion-models.ts` is the proposed home for the first four, beside `stochastic/sde.ts`.
- Energy-based models are split with `feedforward-networks` (Hopfield and RBMs there, the continuous EBMs here) along the line of what the model is for (memory against density); the two should share one energy contract when the library gets `nn/blocks/energy.ts`.
- Every page is 2-D or 8-pixel images on the main thread; images of any size wait on the worker producer.
