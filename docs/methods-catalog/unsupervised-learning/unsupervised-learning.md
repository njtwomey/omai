# Unsupervised learning (part 5, learning paradigms)

Structure without labels: clustering (k-means and its variants, k-medoids, fuzzy c-means, hierarchical, DBSCAN, OPTICS and HDBSCAN, mean shift, spectral and graph-based, affinity propagation, Gaussian mixtures by EM and variational inference), dimensionality reduction (PCA and its variants, factor analysis, ICA, NMF, dictionary learning, PLS and CCA, random projections, kernel PCA), manifold learning (MDS, Isomap, LLE, spectral embeddings, t-SNE, UMAP, self-organising maps), density estimation (histograms, kernels, mixtures), density-ratio estimation and covariance estimation (shrinkage, robust, sparse inverse), as sessions over `cluster`, `mixture`, `numerics/linalg`, `numerics/spatial`, `kernels`, `graph`, `optim` and `stats`. Topic models are `probabilistic-inference`'s, normalising flows `generative-models`', and word and graph embeddings `representation-learning`'s; each has a `see` row below.

## Structure

Clustering is a task, so it lives under `fit/cluster/` (section 3 of `methods/README.md`), one folder per family; dimensionality reduction and manifold learning under `fit/reduce/`; density estimation under `fit/density/`. Covariance estimation is done to data rather than fitted as a model, so it lives under `analyse/statistics/covariance/`; the robust covariance it provides is what `fit/detect/` draws its elliptic envelope from. The choice of k and the validity indices are studies over the clusterers and go to `fit/cluster/validity/` (an `analyse/evaluate/` candidate, recorded under Problems).

```
fit/cluster/
  centres/            k-means (built) with Lloyd and mini-batch as modes, bisecting and kernel k-means, k-medoids, fuzzy c-means
  mixtures/           Gaussian mixtures by EM (built) and by variational inference (built), other emission families
  hierarchical/       agglomerative with every linkage and the dendrogram, BIRCH
  density/            DBSCAN, OPTICS and HDBSCAN, mean shift
  graph/              spectral clustering, affinity propagation, community detection on a neighbour graph
  constrained/        must-link and cannot-link constraints: COP-k-means
  validity/           the choice of k and the internal and external indices as studies over the clusterers
fit/reduce/
  linear/             PCA (SVD, randomised, incremental, sparse), probabilistic PCA and factor analysis, ICA, NMF, dictionary learning, LSA
  cross/              CCA and PLS on two views
  kernel/             kernel PCA
  manifold/           MDS, Isomap, LLE, spectral embeddings and diffusion maps, t-SNE, UMAP, PaCMAP, self-organising maps, embedding quality
fit/density/
  classical/          histograms, kernel density estimation with its bandwidth, mixtures as densities
  ratio/              density ratios by a classifier, KLIEP and uLSIF
analyse/statistics/covariance/   shrinkage, robust (MCD), the graphical lasso
shared/               the planted data slots, the initialisation and distance slots, the neighbour graph (below)
```

The built `methods/cluster/kmeans.ts` moves to `fit/cluster/centres/`, `methods/mixture/gmm.ts` and `bgmm.ts` to `fit/cluster/mixtures/` (`bgmm` is also the CAVI exemplar of `infer/variational/`); `methods/mixture/moe.ts` is `supervised-learning`'s (`fit/regress/experts/`). The random projection of `simulate/numerics/linear-algebra/low-rank/` is `mathematics`' and has a `see` row.

## Methods

Tags on tracks: `s` a per-step scalar, `t` a per-step tensor at summary detail, `t!` a tensor kept in full, `p` a probe computed from the state at any step, `proj` a projection over the tape, `e` an event, `m` a message with a payload. Every clusterer on the iterative base logs `fit/iteration/end` e, its solver's channels as `solver/...`, `model/params` p and `model/assignments` p; every transformer `model/embedding` p; the tables name what a method adds. The status is `built <path>` (with the modes built), `partly` or empty; library readiness is in the needs column (a function named there exists; `TODO:` names what is missing and its proposed home).

## Problems

- `cluster/` and `mixture/` move here as the two built groups; `embedding/` is `representation-learning`'s, `covariance/` goes to `analyse/statistics/`.
- The library gap is wide but each piece is small: `lib/cluster` gains hierarchical, density-based, exemplar and constrained clustering; `lib/decomposition` (PCA, ICA, NMF, cross-decomposition) and `lib/manifold` (MDS, Isomap, LLE, t-SNE, UMAP, SOM, quality) are new, with the spatial, graph and linear-algebra pieces they need already there. `kernelPca` sits in `kernels/matrices.ts` and `lsa` in `text/representations.ts`; both could be re-exported from `lib/decomposition` once it exists.
- `graph/community.ts` exports a `labelPropagationAlgorithm` that is community detection; the semi-supervised label propagation of `semi-supervised-learning` must take another name.
- The validity indices are studies over clusterers and may belong to `analyse/evaluate/` rather than `fit/cluster/validity/`; the folder is kept here because every clusterer's page needs the probe.
- Covariance estimation could equally be a `fit/density/` matter (a Gaussian fitted well); it is filed under `analyse/statistics/` because its users (`fit/detect/`, `pgm`) take the matrix, not a model.
