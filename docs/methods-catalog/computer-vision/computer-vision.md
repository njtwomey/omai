# Computer vision (part 7, application domains)

Classical vision watched (six methods exist): image processing (filters, anisotropic diffusion, total variation, morphology, histograms, pyramids), edges and lines, features and matching, multi-view geometry (homographies, epipolar geometry, calibration, pose, stereo, two-view reconstruction), segmentation (thresholds, superpixels, active contours, GrabCut, watershed, graph cuts), tracking (Lucas-Kanade, optical flow, mean shift, SORT, background models) and recognition without a network (boosting over Haar features, cascades, HOG detectors, bags of visual words, non-maximum suppression), as sessions over `vision`, `signal/image`, `graphics`, `optim`, `stats` (RANSAC) and the geometric scenes of `datasets`. Convolutional networks for recognition and segmentation are `convolutional-networks`'.

## Structure

Everything here is an operation on an image or a sequence of images watched, so it lives under `process/images/` (section 3 of `methods/README.md`), one folder per group. The six built methods move from `methods/vision/` into their groups; their two helpers become `shared/scenes.ts` (the image slot with its truths) and `methods/shared/pictures.ts` (an image as a surface with things drawn over it, which any chapter with a 2-D field uses).

```
process/images/
  filtering/        linear and nonlinear filters, anisotropic diffusion (built), total variation, inpainting, morphology, histograms and colour, pyramids and blending, seam carving
  edges-and-lines/  Canny by stages, Hough lines and circles, line segments
  features/         corners, scale-space keypoints and descriptors, matching, texture descriptors, template matching
  geometry/         homographies and panoramas, epipolar geometry, calibration, pose, stereo, structure from motion
  segmentation/     thresholds, superpixels (built), active contours (built), GrabCut (built), watershed and region growing, mean shift, graph cuts, contours and shapes
  tracking/         KLT (built), dense optical flow, mean-shift and CAMShift tracking, SORT, background subtraction
  recognition/      Haar boosting (built), cascades, HOG detectors, bags of visual words, non-maximum suppression and anchors
  neural-fields/    implicit neural representations (encodings, SIREN, hash grids), NeRF, 3-D Gaussian splatting
  shared/           scenes, pictures, quality probes, the two-image and accumulator records (below)
```

## Methods

Tags on tracks: `s` a per-step scalar, `t` a per-step tensor at summary detail, `t!` a tensor kept in full, `p` a probe computed from the state at any step, `proj` a projection over the tape, `e` an event, `m` a message with a payload. The step of a filter or a segmentation is one iteration of its algorithm; the step of a tracker is one frame; the step of a detector is one scale, one stage or one box; where the library routine runs whole, the step is the knob swept (a radius, a threshold, a level). Pictures follow the built six: the image on the tape in full, a `.../picture` probe that draws the state over it.
