# Feedforward networks (part 6, neural architectures)

The multilayer perceptron and its training: perceptrons and the XOR problem, activations, initialisation, backpropagation watched, optimisers and schedules, regularisation (weight decay, dropout, early stopping, augmentation), normalisation layers, the loss landscape and the phenomena of training (dying units, sharpness, neural collapse), uncertainty from networks (probabilistic heads, ensembles, dropout, SWAG, Laplace), energy-based networks (Hopfield, restricted Boltzmann machines) and compression (pruning, quantisation, distillation), as sessions over `nn`, `optim`, `losses`, `autodiff` and the trainer base. This chapter owns the trainer base's exemplar (a small network on a planted problem) and the training studies every other part-6 chapter reuses.

## Structure

Everything here is a problem method of the torch world (section 7.2 of `methods/README.md`): the network and its objective are the method, the optimiser, schedule, batch size and logging stay generic. It lives under `train/networks/`, one folder per group; the convolutional chapter is its `convolutional/` sibling. The trainer base itself is not this chapter's: it is `methods/shared/bases/trainer.ts` (section 4, planned), the loop of `nn/training/fit.ts` with the optimiser and schedule slots of `methods/shared/options/`, parameter checkpoints for probes and held-out metrics at a schedule; the `mlp` row below is the exemplar that proves it (section 8).

```
train/networks/
  foundations/      the perceptron and XOR, universal approximation, activations, the multilayer perceptron (the trainer base's exemplar), backpropagation by hand
  training/         the optimiser race, schedules and the learning-rate finder, initialisation, normalisation, batch size, gradient noise and clipping
  regularisation/   weight decay against L2, dropout, early stopping, augmentation and label smoothing, the three-session study
  phenomena/        dying units, loss-landscape slices with sharpness and the edge of stability, mode connectivity, neural collapse; double descent and grokking as see rows
  uncertainty/      probabilistic heads, deep ensembles, MC dropout, SWAG, Laplace and Bayes by backprop, evidential heads
  energy-based/     Hopfield recall and dense associative memory, restricted Boltzmann machines by contrastive divergence
  compression/      pruning and lottery tickets, quantisation-aware training, distillation
  shared/           the planted-problem slot, the architecture space, the per-layer probes, landscape probes, head records (below)
  convolutional/    the convolutional-networks chapter
```

## Methods

Tags on tracks: `s` a per-step scalar, `t` a per-step tensor at summary detail, `t!` a tensor kept in full, `p` a probe computed from the state at any step, `proj` a projection over the tape, `e` an event, `m` a message with a payload. The step of a trained row is one optimiser update (a minibatch); the trainer base logs `train/loss`, `train/gradNorm` and `train/lr` every step, `eval/<metric>` at its schedule, and keeps `model/params` as checkpoints so every probe answers at any step. Every row that trains needs the trainer base (`TODO: methods/shared/bases/trainer.ts` over `nn/training/fit.ts`: `fitAlgorithm`, whose channels are `loss`, `gradNorm`, `lr`, `valLoss` and the tensors `params`, `grads`, `activations`); the needs column lists what the row adds to it. Nothing in this chapter is built.

## Examples to make

- A two-layer network on two moons: the decision surface forming, the hidden units' lines as a probe, the loss and held-out accuracy on the tape (the trainer base's exemplar).
- Backpropagation on a three-neuron network, one value per step, the autodiff check beside it.
- Dying ReLUs: the fraction of dead units against the learning rate, leaky ReLU as the fix.
- Weight decay against dropout against early stopping on a noisy fit, one page, three sessions on the same keys.
- Sharpness hovering at 2/η: full-batch gradient descent on a small network, the top eigenvalue and the threshold on one axis.
- Hopfield recall of corrupted digits, the energy falling update by update; capacity failing as patterns are added.
- A lottery ticket: iterative magnitude pruning to 95% sparsity, the rewound subnetwork against a random reinit.
