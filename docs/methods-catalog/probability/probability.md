# Probability (part 1, mathematical foundations)

Probability simulated: sampling by transformation and rejection (inverse transform, Box-Muller, the alias method, Gumbel-max, quasi-random sequences), Monte Carlo estimation and variance reduction (importance, control variates, antithetic, stratified, the gradient estimators), limit theorems and concentration, stochastic processes and stochastic calculus (walks, chains, queues, branching, point processes, Brownian motion, Itô, SDE schemes, diffusions, Fokker-Planck), and the classic problems against their exact answers, as sessions over `random`, `dists`, `sampling`, `stochastic` and `foundations/probability.ts`. Markov chain Monte Carlo as inference, sequential Monte Carlo and the sampler diagnostics live in `probabilistic-inference`.

## Structure

Everything here is a process or a procedure simulated with its truth beside it, so it lives under `simulate/stochastic/` (section 3 of `methods/README.md`), one folder per group; the samplers that answer a query about a model are `infer/samplers/` and appear here as `see` rows. Monte Carlo integration by dimension is shared with `mathematics` (`simulate/numerics/quadrature/`); here it is the estimator and its variance. The built `methods/probability/transformSampling.ts` moves to `sampling/` and `methods/stochastic/diffusionPaths.ts` to `processes/diffusions/`.

```
simulate/stochastic/
  sampling/           transformation (built), rejection and its envelope, Box-Muller and Marsaglia, alias and Gumbel-max, quasi-random sequences
  monte-carlo/        the plain estimate and its 1/√n, importance sampling, variance reduction, the gradient estimators
  limits/             the law of large numbers, the central limit theorem, concentration bounds against tails, extremes
  processes/
    walks-and-chains/ random walks, Markov chains to stationarity, continuous-time chains, branching, queues, point processes, urns and random measures
    diffusions/       Brownian motion and its functionals, Itô calculus, SDE schemes and their orders, diffusions (built), Fokker-Planck, fractional and Lévy
  classics/           birthday, Monty Hall, gambler's ruin, coupon collector, the secretary, Buffon's needle, the dishonest casino
  shared/             the target and proposal slots, the estimate-with-error record, the path bundle record, the exact laws (below)
```

## Methods

Tags on tracks: `s` a per-step scalar, `t` a per-step tensor at summary detail, `t!` a tensor kept in full, `p` a probe computed from the state at any step, `proj` a projection over the tape, `e` an event, `m` a message with a payload. The step of a sampler is a batch of draws (as `transformSampling` does); the step of a process is one time step or one event; the step of a limit theorem is one more sample or one doubling of n. Every estimator row shares `estimate/value` s, `estimate/error` s (the truth known) and `estimate/band` s (the Monte Carlo standard error), named as the `shared/estimate.ts` record names them.

## Examples to make

- Rejection sampling with the proposal envelope and the accepted and rejected points accumulating, M as the knob.
- Importance sampling with a mismatched proposal: the weights' distribution and the effective sample size collapsing, k-hat beside it.
- The central limit theorem: the standardised sum's histogram sharpening into a Gaussian as n doubles, for any family, the exact convolution beside it.
- Brownian paths with the √t envelope and the quadratic variation on the tape; an SDE's bundle against Fokker-Planck's density.
- A Markov chain's distribution converging to stationarity, the total variation and the spectral-gap rate on the tape.
- Gambler's ruin: many fortunes as paths, the ruin probability against the exact curve as p moves.
