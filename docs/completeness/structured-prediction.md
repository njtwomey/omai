# Structured prediction and sequence labelling (CRFs and their neighbours)

Part of the [completeness survey](README.md). sklearn-crfsuite, python-crfsuite (CRFsuite) and pytorch-crf (introspected from the installed packages), and from memory CRF++, AllenNLP and flair (BiLSTM-CRF taggers), pystruct (structured SVMs and the structured perceptron), seqeval (entity-level scores) and the structured-prediction literature (n-best and constrained decoding, semi-Markov, skip-chain and higher-order CRFs, latent and partial-label CRFs, CRF layers on neural encoders, CRF-as-RNN, MEMMs, parsers). The source of omai was read for what each item corresponds to.

Counts are of names: "covered" means an omai export or a documented configuration does what the reference name does, "partial" means a different shape or fewer options, "absent" means nothing found by name or by concept, and "out of scope" is plumbing, GPU or production features, downloads and the like that omai should not copy. Semantics were not compared.

## Where omai is already strong

- Linear-chain CRFs in both the CRFsuite and CRF++ forms: score, log-likelihood, marginals, Viterbi, posterior decoding, feature counts and expected counts, template-driven features from all three template languages (CRF++, Wapiti, CRFsuite) through one extractor, L1 and L2 penalties.
- Training through one solver slot over a small contract (`StructuredOracle`): L-BFGS, every generic smooth solver, OWL-QN for L1, stochastic gradient, the perceptron, averaged perceptron, passive-aggressive and AROW, and the max-margin learners (subgradient, Frank-Wolfe, one-slack and n-slack cutting planes), on the same tape, which neither CRFsuite nor pystruct offer side by side.
- CRFs beyond the chain, all in `pgm/structured`: trees, grids, skip chains, order-k chains and arbitrary graphs with tied weights, semi-Markov CRFs, latent and partial-label CRFs, the MEMM as the label-bias foil, with exact inference where the graph allows it and loopy or tree-reweighted BP where not; MAP by junction trees, MPLP, graph cuts, alpha-expansion and AD3.
- Constrained, n-best and sampled decoding; the CRF layer over a neural encoder (checked against pytorch-crf) and the BiLSTM-CRF tagger; seqeval-style entity metrics (BIO, IOB1, BIOES, BILOU; strict and lenient; micro, macro and per type), scheme-violation counts and `sequenceAccuracyScore`; CKY, Earley, PCFG training, arc-standard and Eisner dependency parsing.

As of 0.1.12 the ring around the CRF is built (decoding, neural layer and tagger, structured SVMs, MEMM, semi-Markov, latent and partial-label CRFs, graph-cut and AD3 MAP) and everything lives in `pgm/structured` and `pgm/inference`. What is still missing: dense CRFs with Gaussian-kernel potentials and mean-field layers (CRF-as-RNN), latent and partial-label models on graphs (they exist for chains), a latent SSVM, user-defined segment features for the semi-Markov CRF, a biaffine dependency parser, a pretrained-embedding or character encoder for the tagger, and full QPBO (probing and improvement).
