# Structured prediction and sequence labelling (CRFs and their neighbours)

Part of the [completeness survey](README.md). sklearn-crfsuite, python-crfsuite (CRFsuite) and pytorch-crf (introspected from the installed packages), and from memory CRF++, AllenNLP and flair (BiLSTM-CRF taggers), pystruct (structured SVMs and the structured perceptron), seqeval (entity-level scores) and the structured-prediction literature (n-best and constrained decoding, semi-Markov, skip-chain and higher-order CRFs, latent and partial-label CRFs, CRF layers on neural encoders, CRF-as-RNN, MEMMs, parsers). The source of omai was read for what each item corresponds to.

Counts are of names: "covered" means an omai export or a documented configuration does what the reference name does, "partial" means a different shape or fewer options, "absent" means nothing found by name or by concept, and "out of scope" is plumbing, GPU or production features, downloads and the like that omai should not copy. Semantics were not compared.

## Where omai is already strong

- Linear-chain CRFs in both the CRFsuite and CRF++ forms: score, log-likelihood, marginals, Viterbi, posterior decoding, feature counts and expected counts, template-driven features (CRF++ and Wapiti syntax), L1 and L2 penalties.
- Training through a solver slot: L-BFGS, every generic smooth solver, stochastic gradient, and three online algorithms of structured prediction (perceptron, averaged perceptron, passive-aggressive with cost-augmented decoding) on the same tape, which neither CRFsuite nor pystruct offer side by side.
- General CRFs on arbitrary graphs with exact or loopy-BP marginals; Gaussian MRFs, factor graphs, HMM, HSMM and IO-HMM, mean-field and MPLP inference.
- seqeval-style entity metrics (BIO, IOB1, BIOES, BILOU; strict and lenient; micro, macro and per type) and scheme-violation counts; CKY, Earley, PCFG training, arc-standard and Eisner dependency parsing.

CRFs are a strength, not a gap. What is still missing is a ring around them: constrained and n-best decoding (a decoder that cannot emit I-X after O), a CRF layer over a neural encoder with gradients into the encoder (the BiLSTM-CRF), a max-margin structured SVM beside the perceptron, the locally normalised MEMM as the foil that teaches label bias, semi-Markov CRFs, latent and partial-label CRFs, and graph-cut or AD3-style MAP for pairwise models. Entity-level metrics, which one might expect to be missing, are present (`metrics/sequence-labelling.ts`). An sklearn-crfsuite-shaped estimator (`fit`, `predict`, `predict_marginals`, `score` on lists of feature dictionaries) is also absent as a wrapper, though every part is there.

Priority is high, medium or low by how many learners would expect the thing, how many pages it unlocks and how well it shows off a stepped, traced run; not by recency. Sizes: S a day or less, M a few days, L a week or more. Homes follow `docs/structure.md`. "Nearest in omai" is what a user would reach for today.
