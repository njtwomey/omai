# Tabular, interpretable and feature libraries; diagnostic charts

Part of the [completeness survey](README.md). interpret and imodels (explainable boosting, rule lists, optimal trees), category_encoders and feature-engine (encoders and feature creation), mlxtend, scikit-multilearn, scikit-learn-extra, umap-learn, hdbscan, TriMap and PaCMAP, yellowbrick and scikit-plot (diagnostic charts), LIME, and the feature set of XGBoost, LightGBM and CatBoost. Introspected: LIME. Everything else is from memory.

Counts are of names: "covered" means an omai export or a documented configuration does what the reference name does, "partial" means a different shape or fewer options, "absent" means nothing found by name or by concept, and "out of scope" is plumbing, GPU or production features, downloads and the like that omai should not copy. Semantics were not compared.

## Where omai is already strong

- Glass-box models: explainable boosting machines (cyclic boosting with pairwise terms), rule lists (CORELS, Bayesian rule lists, CN2, PRISM, RIPPER, OneR), RuleFit, decision tables, and `rules` as a folder of its own.
- Dimension reduction and clustering: t-SNE, UMAP, TriMap, PaCMAP, HDBSCAN with the condensed tree, OPTICS reachability, k-medoids and CLARA.
- Diagnostics: ROC, PR, calibration, confusion matrix, learning curve, residuals, decision regions, scree and biplot, importance, partial dependence and ICE, regularisation paths, threshold sweeps, reliability and conformal views.
- Model comparison: McNemar, Cochran Q, the 5x2 cv F test, bootstrap .632, bias-variance decomposition; stacking and voting; sequential feature selection, RFE, mRMR, stability selection, knockoffs.

The recurring gaps are small: categorical encoders (WoE, CatBoost-style ordered statistics, Helmert and sum contrasts), feature filters (constant, duplicate and correlated columns, probe features), calendar features, monotone constraints and DART or GOSS in the boosters, FIGS and hierarchical shrinkage for interpretable trees, multi-label learners (ML-kNN, RAkEL) and a handful of charts (silhouette, jointplot, cumulative gain and lift).
