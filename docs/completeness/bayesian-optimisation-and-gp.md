# Bayesian optimisation, Gaussian processes, Pyro and BlackJAX

Part of the [completeness survey](README.md). BoTorch, scikit-optimize and the GP stacks GPyTorch and GPflow (acquisition functions, multi-output, variational and sparse GPs, likelihoods, kernels), Pyro (guides, ELBO variants, the effect-handler vocabulary, contrib GPs and experimental design) and BlackJAX (the sampler zoo). Introspected: GPyTorch and BlackJAX. BoTorch, GPflow, scikit-optimize and Pyro are from memory.

Counts are of names: "covered" means an omai export or a documented configuration does what the reference name does, "partial" means a different shape or fewer options, "absent" means nothing found by name or by concept, and "out of scope" is plumbing, GPU or production features, downloads and the like that omai should not copy. Semantics were not compared.

## Where omai is already strong

- Gaussian processes: exact regression with any kernel and the marginal likelihood, sparse GPs with SoR, DTC, FITC and VFE, Laplace and EP classification, relevance vector machines, kernels including ARD, spectral mixture, periodic and compositions.
- Bayesian optimisation: EI, log-EI, PI, UCB with a GP surrogate, TPE, CMA-ES, quasi-random samplers, Hyperband, successive halving, pruners, importance by fANOVA.
- Pyro vocabulary: SVI, Trace, mean-field and Renyi ELBOs, auto-guides (normal, delta, multivariate, low-rank, Laplace, IAF), the handlers (trace, replay, block, condition, do, mask, scale, seed, plate, markov, enumerate), `infer_discrete`, SMC and SVGD.
- Samplers: HMC, NUTS, Riemannian HMC, MALA, SGLD, slice, elliptical slice, SMC, tempered SMC, annealed importance sampling, with the whole chain on the tape.

The batch and information-theoretic acquisition functions of BoTorch, multi-output and variational GPs, and a few Pyro features (reparametrisation handlers, enumerated ELBO, optimal experimental design) are the visible gaps. Nested sampling and the stochastic-gradient HMC family (SGHMC, SGNHT) are the BlackJAX samplers a Bayesian course would meet that omai lacks.
