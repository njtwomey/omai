# Adjacent libraries: offline RL, control, change points, optimisation, geometry, algebra, causal discovery, uncertainty, forecasting, recommenders, data checks, games

Part of the [completeness survey](README.md). Libraries that sit next to the other domains of omai and were not in the first pass. Introspected from the installed environment: control, filterpy, ruptures, stumpy, pysindy, cma, pymoo, cvxpy, POT, optax, shapely, SymPy, causal-learn, MAPIE, nashpy, OpenSpiel (game names and algorithm modules), datasketch, pysubgroup, pingouin, pyitlib, npeet, sdeint, numpy (the array namespace, by group). From memory: d3rlpy, imitation, pykalman, nevergrad, trimesh, problog (found to be covered), gcastle, uncertainty-toolbox, neuralforecast, darts, GluonTS, surprise, implicit, LightFM, RecBole, deepchecks, evidently. SymPy and POT have hundreds of names and were judged by group, so they are in the summary and the tables but left out of the headline totals.

Counts are of names: "covered" means an omai export or a documented configuration does what the reference name does, "partial" means a different shape or fewer options, "absent" means nothing found by name or by concept, and "out of scope" is plumbing, GPU or production features, downloads and the like that omai should not copy. Semantics were not compared.

## Where omai is already strong

- Control and filtering: the LTI toolbox of python-control to the last function, LQR, LQG, pole placement, H-infinity, MPC, iLQR, PID tuning, and Kalman, extended, unscented, ensemble and particle filters with smoothers.
- stumpy to the last function (matrix profile in six algorithms, motifs, discords, chains, snippets, FLUSS and FLOSS, MPdist) as stepped algorithms.
- Game theory: OpenSpiel-style CFR, CFR+ and Monte Carlo CFR, sequence-form LPs, exploitability, support and vertex enumeration, Lemke-Howson, fictitious play, replicator dynamics, regret matching, coalition games, auctions and voting.
- Conformal prediction in the MAPIE style: split, cross, jackknife+, CV+, CQR, Mondrian, adaptive, weighted, APS and RAPS.
- Optimisation: optax at its full width (283 of 306 names), POT's exact, entropic, unbalanced and Gromov-Wasserstein solvers, NSGA-II, CMA-ES, differential evolution and swarm methods.
- Recommenders: implicit-feedback ALS, matrix completion, factorisation machines, DeepFM, DLRM, two-tower, BPR and ranking losses, click models, MovieLens.

The recurring gaps in this group are the evaluation and reporting layers (a data-audit report like deepchecks and Evidently, conformal risk control and exchangeability tests from MAPIE), forecasting as a pipeline (windows, transformers, backtests, baselines), neighbourhood and graph collaborative filtering, the classic board-game shelf (chess, Othello, checkers, Hex), multi-objective evolutionary algorithms beyond NSGA-II with the ZDT and DTLZ problems, and optimal-transport domain adaptation.

`sympy` and `pot` were judged by area, not by name; their rows are in the tables but not in the totals of `counts.json` and the README.
