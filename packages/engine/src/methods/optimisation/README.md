# Optimisation (part 1, mathematical foundations)

Optimisers watched on landscapes: gradient, accelerated, coordinate and proximal methods, Newton and quasi-Newton, constrained problems and duality, stochastic gradients and variance reduction, derivative-free and global search, and optimal transport, as sessions over `optim`, `autodiff` and the named objectives of `datasets/objectives.ts`. This chapter is the showcase of two shared slots: `smoothSolvers` (`methods/shared/options/solvers.ts`, the modes every smooth objective admits) and `schedules` (`options/schedules.ts`, the learning-rate shapes a gradient mode takes). An optimiser race is one study over any model whose solver slot is `smoothSolvers`, so the races live in `study/race/`; the same optimisers fill the solver slot of every model elsewhere.

## Structure

An optimiser on a named landscape is a numerical procedure watched, so the single-run methods live under `simulate/numerics/optimisation/` (section 3 of `methods/README.md`), one folder per family, with the method as the mode where a family has several (`first-order` with the step rule as the mode, `line-search` with the rule as the mode). Races and landscape studies are methods over methods and go to `study/race/`, taking the solver slot and an objective slot. Simulated annealing is built in `methods/search/annealing.ts` and stays a `search/local/` method (the `search-and-planning` chapter's); it appears here as a row of the global group so the comparison with basin hopping is a lookup. Bayesian optimisation is `model-selection`'s (`study/sweep/`), and the Sinkhorn iterations are shared with `generative-models` (its transport losses) but compared with the exact plan here.

```
simulate/numerics/optimisation/
  first-order/        gradient descent with its step rules, momentum and Nesterov, coordinate descent, proximal gradient (ISTA, FISTA), mirror descent, subgradient, Frank-Wolfe
  second-order/       line searches watched, Newton with Hessian modification, BFGS, L-BFGS, SR1, trust regions, Gauss-Newton and Levenberg-Marquardt
  constrained/        KKT geometry, penalty and barrier methods, the augmented Lagrangian and ADMM, projected gradient, dual ascent, the simplex path and the central path, active sets
  stochastic/         SGD with the batch as the knob, schedules, variance reduction (SAG, SAGA, SVRG), adaptive optimisers, preconditioning, online learning
  derivative-free/    Nelder-Mead, pattern search, Powell, CMA-ES, differential evolution, particle swarm, basin hopping and dual annealing
  transport/          Sinkhorn against the exact plan, unbalanced transport, barycentres
  shared/             the objective slot (landscapes with their minima and conditioning), the landscape probe, the trajectory record (below)
study/race/
  optimisers/         several solvers of `smoothSolvers` on one objective from one start, the conditioning study, the schedule study, the batch-size study
  landscapes/         the loss landscape of a model on data: slices through the iterate, the Hessian spectrum, the path on the surface
```

The built `methods/search/annealing.ts` stays where it is (`search/local/`); `methods/tune/bayesianOptimisation.ts` goes to `study/sweep/` with `model-selection`. Nothing of this chapter is built yet: its first exemplar is the optimiser race, which is also the first exemplar of the solver slot (section 8 of `methods/README.md`).

## Methods

Tags on tracks: `s` a per-step scalar, `t` a per-step tensor at summary detail, `t!` a tensor kept in full, `p` a probe computed from the state at any step, `proj` a projection over the tape, `e` an event, `m` a message with a payload. The step is one iteration of the solver (one accepted step; the line search inside it is a message). The minimisers of `optim/minimize` carry one schema (`loss`, `gradNorm`, `step`, `nfev`; the tensors `x` and `grad`; the messages `lineSearch` and `curvature`), which the iterative base surfaces as `solver/...` paths, so every row below shares `solver/objective` s, `solver/gradNorm` s, `solver/x` t! (the iterate), `solver/step` s, `solver/lineSearch` m, `model/params` p (the vector reached) and `landscape/surface` p (the objective on a grid over the box, as `annealing` draws it); a row names only what it adds.

## Problems

- The `gradient` mode of `smoothSolvers` (an `optim/transform.ts` chain, a batch and a `schedules` entry) does not exist yet; momentum, Adam, SGD and the schedules are methods of their own above only until it does, and the schedule race and batch-size study wait on it.
- The minimisers' own channel is `loss`, so the iterative base surfaces it as `solver/loss`; the tables follow the settled name `solver/objective`, which means an alias in the base's contract or in `shared/trajectory.ts`.
- A race is one session driving several: the race base, the same-key rule across solvers (problem 6 of `methods/README.md`) and the comparison panel are its prerequisites; `shared/race.ts` is a stopgap until `methods/shared/sweeps.ts` lands.
- `search/annealing` stays with `search-and-planning`; swarm and population search belong there too and appear here only as the `evolution-strategies` row. Bayesian optimisation is `model-selection`'s.
