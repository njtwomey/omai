# Statistics (part 1, mathematical foundations)

Statistics watched: estimators and their sampling distributions, intervals and their coverage, hypothesis tests and their errors, power, multiple testing, sequential tests, Bayesian updating, resampling (the bootstrap, the jackknife, permutation tests), regression diagnostics, survival analysis and experimental design, as sessions over `stats`, `dists`, `linear` and `datasets`. The methods are studies of behaviour (coverage, power, the bootstrap distribution forming, a posterior tightening), not the tests themselves, which are library functions; most repeat a procedure over fresh samples, so the repeated-sample study is this chapter's base.

## Structure

Everything here is done to data, so it lives under `analyse/statistics/` (section 3 of `methods/README.md`), one folder per group. The studies by repetition (coverage, power, a sampling distribution) answer this chapter's own questions, so they stay here rather than in `study/`, built on the repeated-sample base of `shared/repeat.ts` (the sweep base of `methods/shared/sweeps.ts` once it exists). A/B tests and sequential experimentation are `online-experimentation`'s, causal effects from observational data are `causal-inference`'s, and the stationarity and autocorrelation tests are `time-series`'; each appears as a `see` row.

```
analyse/statistics/
  estimation/         sampling distributions, maximum likelihood watched, intervals and their coverage, robust and shrinkage estimators, density estimation
  testing/            a test and its errors, power curves, multiple testing, sequential tests, permutation and two-sample tests, goodness of fit
  bayesian/           conjugate updating one observation at a time, credible intervals, Bayes factors and the ROPE, Bayesian regression (see probabilistic-inference)
  resampling/         the bootstrap and jackknife distributions forming, bootstrap intervals and their coverage, the permutation distribution
  regression/         residual diagnostics, leverage and influence, multicollinearity, heteroscedasticity on a fitted linear model
  survival/           Kaplan-Meier and Nelson-Aalen as events arrive, the log-rank test, Cox proportional hazards fitted iteratively
  design/             randomisation and blocking, factorial designs and ANOVA, A/B tests (see online-experimentation), causal designs (see causal-inference)
  shared/             the repeated-sample base, the distribution slot with its truths, the interval strip record, the test-result record (below)
```

Nothing of this chapter is built; `methods/linear/bayesianRegression.ts` is the conjugate-updating instance and belongs to `probabilistic-inference` (`infer/models/`).

## Methods

Tags on tracks: `s` a per-step scalar, `t` a per-step tensor at summary detail, `t!` a tensor kept in full, `p` a probe computed from the state at any step, `proj` a projection over the tape, `e` an event, `m` a message with a payload. The step of a repeated-sample study is one fresh sample (the estimate, interval or decision it produced), so the tape holds the sampling distribution and the summary over it (coverage, rejection rate) is a projection; the step of an updating method is one observation; the step of a fit is one iteration.

## Examples to make

- Coverage: a hundred confidence intervals drawn as the samples arrive, the ones that miss highlighted, the running coverage against the nominal level.
- Power: the test's rejection rate against the effect size with n as the knob, the closed form beneath.
- A beta posterior updating coin by coin, with the prior's strength as the knob and the predictive beside it.
- The bootstrap distribution of a median filling in, resample by resample, the percentile and BCa intervals marked.
- Kaplan-Meier stepping down as events arrive, censored subjects marked, the true survival function beneath.
- One point dragged across a scatter: leverage, Cook's distance and the fitted line swinging.

## Rendering

- Curves with bands, histograms against densities, a posterior over a grid (`posteriorGrid`), step functions with marks, scatter plots with draggable points (`interaction`).
- Missing: an interval strip (many intervals as horizontal bars against a truth line); a table of test results with the decision rule shown; a QQ and residual-plot pair; a sorted p-value panel with the step-up thresholds.

## Problems

- Studies by repetition need many runs per step (a hundred samples per point of a power curve, a whole bootstrap per sample of a coverage study): `shared/repeat.ts` keeps the inner work off the tape with `subRun`, and is the hardest case for the sweep base (problem 11 of `methods/README.md`).
- The library gaps are three small files: `stats/survival.ts` (Kaplan-Meier, Nelson-Aalen, log-rank, Cox), `linear/diagnostics.ts` (leverage, Cook's distance, VIF, Breusch-Pagan) and `stats/design.ts` (allocations, factorial designs, two-way ANOVA); James-Stein beside them in `stats/shrinkage.ts`.
- The old `survival/` and the statistical half of `experimentation/` move here; the running A/B test belongs to `online-experimentation` and the observational designs to `causal-inference`, each a `see` row above.
