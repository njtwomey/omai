# Trustworthy machine learning (part 8, practice)

Models examined and made trustworthy: interpretability (Shapley values and their estimators, LIME and anchors, gradient attributions for networks, attributions on text, images and series, permutation importance, dependence plots, surrogates and rules, glass-box models, counterfactuals and recourse, prototypes, concepts, data valuation, explanation quality), fairness (group, individual and intersectional measures, bias in data, regression and ranking fairness, mitigation before, during and after training, the frontier and the impossibility, delayed impact), robustness (adversarial examples and training, certified bounds, corruption robustness), privacy (the mechanisms and composition, DP-SGD with its accountant, membership inference and the other attacks), and label noise (its effect, robust objectives, sample selection, cleaning), as sessions over `explain`, `fairness`, `learn` (privacy), `data` (modifiers), `losses`, `metrics`, `nn` and any fitted model of the other chapters. Calibration, conformal prediction and uncertainty are `decision-theory-and-calibration`'s; shift is `shift-and-drift`'s; data selection is `data-centric-learning`'s.

## Structure

Nearly everything here is done to a fitted model or to data, so it lives under `analyse/` (section 3 of `methods/README.md`): `analyse/explain/` and `analyse/fairness/` as the layout names them, `analyse/calibrate/` by reference. Robustness and privacy are not in section 3's list of `analyse/` folders; they belong there because their pages are audits of a fitted model (an attack, a certificate, an inference about the training set) with the training that defends against them (adversarial training, DP-SGD) as a mode of the same page, where the comparison with the clean or non-private model lives; the alternative, the two trainings under `train/networks/`, is recorded under Problems. Label noise is a property of data: the modifier, its effect on any task method and the objectives that resist it are a data study, `data/label-noise/`; the cleaning that selects rows is `data-centric-learning`'s. The fitted-model slot (any method's fitted value as the probe target) is the chapter's shared piece.

```
analyse/explain/
  attributions/         Shapley estimators, local surrogates and anchors, gradient attributions, attributions on text, images and series
  inspection/           permutation and conditional importance, dependence (PDP, ICE, ALE), global surrogates and rules, glass-box models
  examples/             counterfactuals and recourse, prototypes and criticisms, concepts, data valuation
  evaluation/           explanation quality and sanity checks, internal and attention attributions
analyse/fairness/
  measures/             the group audit over a threshold sweep, individual, intersectional, data bias, regression and ranking fairness, counterfactual fairness
  mitigation/           pre-, in- and post-processing with the method as a mode, the frontier and the impossibility
  dynamics/             delayed impact, repeated decisions, disparities under shift
analyse/calibrate/      calibration, conformal prediction and uncertainty: `decision-theory-and-calibration`'s (see rows)
analyse/robustness/     adversarial examples with the attack as a mode, adversarial training, certified robustness, corruption robustness
analyse/privacy/        the mechanisms and composition, DP-SGD with the accountant on the tape, privacy attacks
data/label-noise/       the planted noise and its effect, robust objectives as a slot, sample selection, cleaning
shared/                 the fitted-model slot, the explainer and mitigation slot tables, the sensitive-attribute data with truth, the budget record (below)
```

Nothing of this chapter is built.

## Methods

Tags on tracks: `s` a per-step scalar, `t` a per-step tensor at summary detail, `t!` a tensor kept in full, `p` a probe computed from the state at any step, `proj` a projection over the tape, `e` an event, `m` a message with a payload. The step of an explainer that iterates is one sample, one permutation, one candidate or one gradient step; the step of an audit over thresholds is one threshold; the step of a mitigation is one iteration of its reduction or one repair level; the step of an attack is one gradient step; the step of a training is one minibatch. Every explanation method takes the fitted-model slot (`shared/model.ts`) and a chosen row, and logs the explanation's own convergence where there is one.

## Problems

- The library gap is three files: `lib/nn/adversarial.ts` (the attacks, randomised smoothing, interval bounds), `lib/learn/privacy-attacks.ts` (membership and the other inferences) and `lib/learn/noisy-labels.ts` (the robust losses' corrections, co-teaching, confident learning), plus the per-example DP transformation in `learn/privacy.ts`. Everything in `explain` and `fairness` exists; what is missing there is the stepped form of a few routines (`exponentiatedGradientReduction`, `mmdCritic`, `explainableBoosting`).
- Where the two trainings live: `adversarial-training` and `dp-sgd` are rows of the audit folders here because their page is the comparison with the undefended model; they are also modes of the trainer base, and `train/networks/` could carry a `see` row to them (or the reverse). Decide when the trainer base exists.
- The trees that `global-surrogates-and-rules` and tree SHAP need are `supervised-learning`'s and not in `lib`; those rows run on the models that exist until then.
- Label noise sits between this chapter and `data-centric-learning` (which selects rows) and `weak-supervision` (which models label quality); the split here is by question (what noise does, and what resists it, against which rows to keep).
- Calibration, conformal prediction and uncertainty are `decision-theory-and-calibration`'s; the `see` rows under `analyse/calibrate/` and `analyse/robustness/` point there, and the per-group forms are its Mondrian and per-group modes rather than copies here.
