# Scientific machine learning (part 6, neural architectures)

Learning with physics: physics-informed neural networks (the residual of an ODE or PDE as the loss, boundary and initial terms, collocation points, loss balancing, inverse problems), learning dynamical systems from data (neural ODEs and their variants, the adjoint against backpropagation, Hamiltonian networks, neural SDEs, latent ODEs), operator learning (DeepONet and Fourier neural operators on function pairs), equation discovery (SINDy's sparse regression over a library, DMD and Koopman, symbolic regression, universal differential equations) and surrogates of simulators (Gaussian-process emulators, active sampling), as sessions over `nn`, `dynamics`, `autodiff`, `losses/physics`, `symbolic` and the trainer base, with the named equations of `dynamics` as the truth.

## Structure

Problem methods of the torch world (section 7.2 of `methods/README.md`): the equation, the network and the loss are the method, the optimiser and schedule generic, so the chapter lives under `train/scientific/`, a folder of `train/` that section 3 does not list yet (recorded under Problems). The solvers' own behaviour (order, stability, step control) is `mathematics`' `simulate/numerics/ode/` and `pde/`; the named systems' dynamics are `dynamics`' `simulate/dynamics/`; this chapter uses both as the truth it learns against. SINDy and DMD are fits rather than trained networks and could sit in `fit/regress/`; they stay here because their comparison with the truth (the recovered equation against the generating one) is this chapter's.

```
train/scientific/
  pinns/            the physics-informed network on a named equation, collocation, loss balancing, hard constraints and Fourier features, inverse problems, variants
  neural-odes/      the neural ODE on trajectories, adjoint against backpropagation, solver and tolerance studies, augmented and second-order, Hamiltonian, latent and controlled, neural SDEs
  operators/        DeepONet and the Fourier neural operator on function pairs, against dense baselines
  discovery/        SINDy with the library and the threshold, DMD and Koopman, symbolic regression, universal differential equations
  surrogates/       Gaussian-process and neural emulators of a simulator, active sampling, multi-fidelity
  shared/           the equation slot with reference solutions, collocation, the field and trajectory probes, the equation-against-truth record (below)
```

## Methods

Tags on tracks: `s` a per-step scalar, `t` a per-step tensor at summary detail, `t!` a tensor kept in full, `p` a probe computed from the state at any step, `proj` a projection over the tape, `e` an event, `m` a message with a payload. Trained rows step one optimiser update on the trainer base (`train/loss`, `train/gradNorm`, `train/lr`, `eval/<metric>`, `model/params` as checkpoints; `TODO: methods/shared/bases/trainer.ts` over `nn/training/fit.ts`: `fitAlgorithm`), with the loss's physics terms logged apart (`train/residual`, `train/boundary`, `train/data`); the truth is the equation's exact solution where it has one and a fine-tolerance numerical one otherwise, which the `eval/error` track says (`eval/errorAgainst` m). Nothing in this chapter is built.

## Problems

- `train/scientific/` is a folder section 3 of `methods/README.md` does not list; it should be added to the `train/` line (the alternative, `train/networks/scientific/`, hides a chapter whose models are mostly not plain networks).
- Reference solutions come from `dynamics` solvers at fine tolerance where no closed form exists; every error track says which truth it is measured against (`eval/errorAgainst`), and the pages mark numerical truths as such.
- The library has the losses, the operators, the ODE blocks, the adjoint, SINDy, DMD and the operator datasets; it lacks the operator blocks (`nn/blocks/operators.ts`), the Hamiltonian block, a stepped SINDy, the causal and variational PINN pieces and the multi-fidelity GP, each named above.
- Training through an ODE solver on the main thread is the slowest thing in the methods layer; the pages are small fields on short horizons until the worker producer exists.
