# Time series (part 7, application domains)

Series modelled and mined: foundations (stationarity, autocorrelation, decomposition), ARMA and ARIMA (identification, fitting, forecasting), exponential smoothing, state-space models (the Kalman filter and smoother, structural models), volatility models (ARCH, GARCH), multivariate series (VAR, cointegration), forecasting practice (backtesting, horizons), change-point detection, the matrix-profile family (motifs, discords, chains, segmentation, snippets), time-series classification (shapelets, kernels, dictionaries) and neural forecasting, as sessions over `signal`, `stats`, `dynamics`, `nn` and the series algorithms moving to `lib`.

## Structure

Forecasting is a task, so the models live under `fit/forecast/` (section 3 of `methods/README.md`), one folder per family, with the fit as a mode where a family has several (`arima`: conditional sum of squares, exact likelihood by the Kalman filter, innovations). Mining a series (its profile, its change points, its components) is an operation on a signal watched, so it lives under `process/signals/series/`; classifying series is `fit/classify/series/`. The three share one series slot (`fit/forecast/shared/series.ts`), so the split is a lookup, not a boundary; the alternative, one `fit/forecast/` root with a `mine/` folder, is recorded under Problems.

```
fit/forecast/
  processes/
    ar/                 Yule-Walker, Burg, least squares and maximum likelihood; the order by PACF and AIC
    ma/                 the innovations algorithm; invertibility
    arma/               identification (ACF, PACF, EACF), prediction-error and exact fits
    arima/              differencing, the fit as a mode (CSS, exact ML by Kalman, innovations), forecasts with intervals
    sarima/             seasonal differencing and polynomials, exogenous regressors (SARIMAX), automatic orders
    var/                vector autoregression: Granger causality, impulse responses, variance decomposition
    cointegration/      Engle-Granger and Johansen; the spread
    volatility/         ARCH, GARCH, EGARCH, GJR: the conditional variance
  smoothing/            simple, Holt, damped, Holt-Winters, ETS as one method with the form as a mode
  state-space/          structural models through the Kalman filter and smoother, parameters by EM, nonlinear and particle filters, Bayesian structural
  practice/             baselines, rolling-origin backtests, horizons, probabilistic scores, conformal intervals, reconciliation
  neural/               recurrent and convolutional forecasters, N-BEATS, probabilistic heads, state-space sequence models
  shared/               the series slot, structural forms, horizons and fans, windows, residual diagnostics (below)
process/signals/series/
  foundations/          autocorrelation, stationarity tests, transforms, the spectrum of a process
  decomposition/        classical, STL, SSA, EMD and VMD
  change-points/        BOCPD, PELT, binary segmentation, CUSUM and Page-Hinkley
  matrix-profile/       STOMP and STUMP, the anytime STAMP and SCRIMP++, streaming STUMPI, motifs and discords, chains, FLUSS and FLOSS, MPdist and snippets, pan profiles, multidimensional
fit/classify/series/    DTW nearest neighbours, shapelets, ROCKET, bags of SAX words, feature sets
```

The built `methods/dynamics/kalmanTracking.ts` is a filter following a moving point and goes to `infer/filters/`; the structural models here reuse its filter on their own matrices.

## Methods

Tags on tracks: `s` a per-step scalar, `t` a per-step tensor at summary detail, `t!` a tensor kept in full, `p` a probe computed from the state at any step, `proj` a projection over the tape, `e` an event, `m` a message with a payload. The step of a fit is an iteration of its solver; the step of a filter, a smoother, a streaming profile or an online detector is one observation; the step of an order search or a backtest is one trial or one origin.

## Problems

- The library gap is concentrated: the matrix-profile loops (`stomp`, `stump`, `stamp`, `scrimp`, `stumpi`, `fluss`, `floss`, discovery) over kernels that exist, and the ARIMA family's state-space forms (seasonal, exact likelihood, innovations) over a Kalman filter that exists. Both are one `lib/timeseries/` and one `lib/signal/matrix-profile.ts` away; GARCH, Holt-Winters, STL and VAR are small additions beside them.
- The split between `fit/forecast/` and `process/signals/series/`: one series slot serves both, so the alternative (one `fit/forecast/` root with a `mine/` folder) costs nothing to adopt later; decide when the first mining method is built.
- Several filters (`bocpd`, the particle filters) are `Algorithm`s inside a function that runs them; exposing the algorithm is a one-line change per routine, and the stepped forms of `pelt` and `binarySegmentation` are new work.
- `methods/dynamics/kalmanTracking.ts` goes to `infer/filters/` as the observer page (`control-theory` wants it as well); the structural models here import its filter, not its world.
