# Mathematics (part 1, mathematical foundations)

Mathematics computed, drawn and stepped through: linear algebra, calculus, differential equations, splines, numerical methods, geometry and graphs, as sessions over `numerics`, `autodiff`, `dynamics`, `symbolic`, `graph` and `foundations`. The methods here are studies: a procedure watched (an eigenvalue iteration, Newton's method, an ODE solver choosing its steps) rather than a model fitted.

## Structure

Everything here is a numerical procedure, so it lives under `simulate/numerics/` (section 3 of `methods/README.md`), one folder per group; the graph algorithms are operations on a graph watched, so they live under `process/graphs/`. The behaviour of named dynamical systems (phase portraits, bifurcations, chaos) is the `dynamics` chapter's and goes to `simulate/dynamics/`; only the solvers' own behaviour is here.

```
simulate/numerics/
  linear-algebra/
    eigen/              power, inverse and Rayleigh-quotient iterations, QR iteration, Jacobi, subspace iteration, Lanczos and Arnoldi
    factorisations/     Gaussian elimination, Cholesky, QR (Householder, Givens, Gram-Schmidt), SVD by Jacobi, least squares, conditioning
    krylov/             conjugate gradients, MINRES, GMRES, BiCGSTAB with preconditioners; stationary iterations; two-grid
    low-rank/           randomised SVD, truncated SVD, Hutchinson's trace, random projections
    inverse-problems/   Tikhonov and TSVD with the parameter rules, regularising iterations (Landweber, CGLS, LSQR)
    matrix-functions/   the exponential by scaling and squaring, the square root by Denman-Beavers
  calculus/             Riemann sums, Taylor polynomials, finite differences against autodiff, forward against reverse mode
  roots/                scalar methods, Newton basins, systems (Newton, Broyden), continuation
  quadrature/           Newton-Cotes and Romberg, Gauss rules, adaptive subdivision, Monte Carlo and quasi-Monte Carlo
  interpolation/        polynomial (Runge), splines, Bezier, best approximation (Remez, chebfun), RBF, acceleration, floating point
  ode/                  fixed-step Runge-Kutta, adaptive pairs, multistep, stiff solvers, symplectic, boundary values, sensitivities
  pde/                  finite differences with stability, conservation laws, spectral, finite elements
  geometry/             rotations, a linear map on a grid, curves, surfaces and geodesics, hulls and triangulations, conformal maps, hyperbolic
  number-theory/        sieve, Euclid, modular arithmetic, primality, factorisation, continued fractions, series and Fourier series
  shared/               the catalogue of functions, matrices, systems and integrands with their truths (below)
process/graphs/
  traversal/            BFS and DFS as a board
  paths/                Dijkstra, A*, Bellman-Ford, Floyd-Warshall
  trees-and-flows/      Kruskal and Prim, augmenting paths and the min cut
  centrality/           PageRank and HITS as power iterations, eigenvector and Katz centrality
  colouring/            greedy colouring, cliques, components, topological order
```

The built `methods/linalg/eigenIterations.ts` moves to `simulate/numerics/linear-algebra/eigen/` and `methods/dynamics/odeSystems.ts` to `simulate/numerics/ode/` (its named systems stay a slot shared with `simulate/dynamics/`).

## Methods

Tags on tracks: `s` a per-step scalar, `t` a per-step tensor at summary detail, `t!` a tensor kept in full, `p` a probe computed from the state at any step, `proj` a projection over the tape, `e` an event, `m` a message with a payload. The status is `built <path>`, `lib ready` (every piece named exists), `lib partly` (named pieces exist, the stepped form or one routine is missing) or `lib missing` (the algorithm is not in the library).

## Problems

- Where a study ends and the library's own example begins: a `numerics` function already has an `@example`; the study adds the watching, and should not duplicate the docs.
- Many library routines return the whole answer with a history (`roots/scalar.ts`, `quadrature/adaptive.ts`, `jacobiEigenSteps`, `gramSchmidt`) rather than a stepped `Algorithm`; replaying a history is enough for a page, but a session that forks at a step needs the stepped form. Which routines get one (Lanczos, Cholesky, shortest paths, max flow) is the first library work this chapter asks for.
- `linalg/` (eigen iterations) and `foundations/` move here; `dynamics/odeSystems` is shared with `control-theory`.
