# Curiosities (part 9)

Mechanisms worth watching for their own sake: epicycles tracing a drawing, Kepler's equation solved by Newton, orbits and Lagrange points integrated, light bending around a mass, the Cole-Hopf transformation turning Burgers' equation linear, the Kelvin-Helmholtz instability on a lattice, Keplerian shear, as sessions over `dynamics`, `numerics`, `foundations` and `graphics`. Each curiosity is a procedure with a knob or two and a striking picture.

## Capabilities

- Each curiosity as a procedure method: its state on the tape, its picture as a probe, its one or two knobs.
- The catalogue grows by one file per curiosity; nothing is shared but the procedure base and the pictures.

## Examples to make

- Epicycles redrawing a path from its Fourier coefficients, the number of terms as the knob (the gallery had this).
- Kepler's equation by Newton for a chosen eccentricity, the iterate on the orbit.
- The restricted three-body problem with a test mass near a Lagrange point.
- Light rays bending around a mass by integrating the geodesic equation.
